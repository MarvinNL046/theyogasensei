# Stories — recurring anecdotes for theyogasensei.com

Real, repeatable, honest stories from testing products and building a yoga practice. **One story per post, max.** Never invent new ones — every story here must have actually happened. Stories that aren't true are the fastest way for readers (and Google's quality classifiers) to register a site as untrustworthy.

Each story has a title, a one-line summary, the full version, and tags so the page-generation skill can find a relevant story for a given article topic.

## Story prompts — what to capture as you go

You'll generate stories naturally as you test gear and build a practice. Watch for these moments and write them down within 24 hours while details are fresh:

### Gear-testing stories that work

- **The first day of using a new product.** What surprised you? What did the marketing get wrong? What did it get right?
- **The product that failed.** A mat that slipped, a block that cracked, an app that froze. Honest failure stories are the most trustworthy thing you can publish.
- **The discovery story.** "I bought this expecting X. After a month I realised Y." The reveal must be genuine.
- **The comparison moment.** Two products tested in the same week, one obviously better — and _why_. Specific, not vague.

### Practice stories that work (even without certification)

- **The "I tried this and got it wrong" story.** Got into a pose wrong, watched a YouTube tutorial, fixed it. Beginner anxiety being real is your best E-E-A-T asset.
- **The unexpected resource.** "I bought a yoga book that turned out to be useless. The free YouTube channel I almost ignored became my daily teacher."
- **The plateau story.** Three weeks of progress, then a wall. What broke through it — for _you_, not "in general".

### Stories that don't work — never write these

- Stories about teaching others (you don't teach).
- Stories about retreats / training you haven't been on.
- Stories that exaggerate a small thing into a transformation.
- Stories that quote dialogue you didn't actually hear.
- Stories that use the word "journey" anywhere in them.

## When the skill picks a story

The page-generation skill reads this file and selects **at most one** story per article, matched by tag. If no story matches strongly, the skill writes the article without one rather than forcing a tangential anecdote in. A forced story is worse than no story.

After publishing, the skill notes which story was used in the article's frontmatter (`storyUsed: <title>`) so we can avoid using the same story twice in the same cluster within 60 days.

---

## Story bank suggestions to write first

Once you have your first mat in hand, the highest-leverage three stories to draft within the first month:

1. **"What the Manduka reviews don't tell you"** (or whichever mat you start with) — the first-week break-in surprise, the salt-and-vinegar treatment, what the colour you picked actually looks like after 20 hours of sweat. ~300 words.

2. **"The block I almost didn't buy"** — first time you needed a block in a pose you couldn't reach. Honest about the moment when you stopped resisting it and realised it was the right call. ~200 words.

3. **"Three weeks, three apps"** — installed Down Dog, Glo, and one free YouTube channel for three weeks each. Which one made you actually show up? ~400 words.

Those three carry you through the first 10 articles of the gear cluster. After that, each new test produces its own story naturally.
