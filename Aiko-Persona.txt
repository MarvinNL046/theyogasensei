Persona Guidelines voor AGENTS.md

Je kunt dit letterlijk toevoegen:

## Visual Persona: The Yoga Sensei Guide

The website uses a recurring fictional visual guide persona. She represents calm discipline, mindful practice, and practical guidance. She should never feel like a stock influencer, celebrity, or fake guru.

### Persona Identity
- Internal prompt name: Aiko
- Public role: The Yoga Sensei Guide
- Purpose: recurring visual brand presence
- Do not present her as a real certified teacher or founder unless explicitly defined as such.

### Visual Traits
- Asian woman, early/mid 30s
- Calm, grounded, focused expression
- Dark hair in a neat bun
- Minimal makeup
- Realistic athletic build
- No flashy jewelry or logos

### Clothing
- Primary: charcoal/black minimalist yoga set
- Secondary: dark olive yoga set or light linen layer
- No bright colors, patterns, or visible brand logos

### Environment
- Japanese-inspired minimalist yoga studio
- Warm wooden floor
- Shoji screens or soft wooden frames
- Cream plaster walls
- Sage green yoga mat
- Ceramic vase, bonsai, olive branch, tea cup, journal
- Warm morning light with soft shadows

### Photography Style
- Premium editorial wellness photography
- Calm, spacious composition
- Natural materials
- Soft depth of field
- Subtle Japanese zen aesthetic
- Avoid stock-photo smiles, influencer poses, neon gym styling, and overly spiritual clichés.

### Consistency Rules
The persona should be recognizable through repeated visual language:
- Same hair bun
- Same outfit palette
- Same calm body language
- Same studio world
- Same warm light
- Same sage mat
- Same premium editorial photography

Do not rely only on face consistency. Back views, side profiles, hands, and practice-focused compositions are encouraged.
