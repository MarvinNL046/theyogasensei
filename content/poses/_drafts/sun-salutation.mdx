faq:
  - q: How many sun salutations should a beginner do?
    a: Start with three rounds and add one per week up to ten. Form matters more than count; ten sloppy rounds undo what three careful rounds build.
  - q: Sun Salutation A or B for beginners?
    a: Start with A. It has twelve clear positions and no chair-pose entries, so the legs do not fatigue before the breath rhythm is set. Move to B after a month of A.
  - q: Can I do Sun Salutation every day?
    a: Yes — it is a complete short practice. Daily Sun Salutation rounds warm the spine, hips, and shoulders in three minutes per round. Skip rest days for the first month to build the habit, then taper to five days per week.
  - q: What is the breath pattern in Sun Salutation?
    a: One breath per position, inhaling on extension and exhaling on flexion. Inhale to Mountain raised, exhale to Forward Fold, inhale to Half Lift, exhale through Chaturanga to Up-Dog, and so on. The breath sets the pace.

  step:
    - name: Tadasana — Mountain Pose
      text: Stand with feet hip-width apart, arms at your sides, palms facing forward. Engage the quadriceps and lift the kneecaps. Lengthen through the crown of the head. Three breaths to settle.
    - name: Urdhva Hastasana — Upward Salute
      text: Inhale and sweep the arms overhead. Palms together, gaze up if the neck allows. Lengthen the spine — do not crunch the lower back.
    - name: Uttanasana — Standing Forward Fold
      text: Exhale and hinge from the hips. Hands to shins, mat, or blocks. Let the head hang. Soften the knees if the hamstrings protest.
    - name: Ardha Uttanasana — Half-Lift
      text: Inhale and lift the chest halfway, lengthening the spine parallel to the floor. Hands to shins. Crown of the head reaches forward.
    - name: Chaturanga Dandasana — Four-Limbed Staff Pose
      text: Exhale and step or float back to a plank, then lower halfway, keeping elbows pinned to the ribs. Modification — drop the knees first.
    - name: Urdhva Mukha Svanasana — Upward-Facing Dog
      text: Inhale and roll over the toes, lifting the chest and thighs off the mat. Shoulders away from the ears. Modification — Cobra (knees and pelvis stay grounded).
    - name: Adho Mukha Svanasana — Downward-Facing Dog
      text: Exhale and tuck the toes, lifting the hips back and up. Spread the fingers wide. Pedal the feet for a few breaths to warm the calves. Hold for five breaths.
    - name: Step or Jump Forward
      text: Inhale and step or hop the feet between the hands. Land softly, knees soft. The mat absorbs the landing — not the joints.
    - name: Ardha Uttanasana — Half-Lift (return)
      text: Inhale and lift the chest halfway again. Lengthen the spine forward, crown reaching.
    - name: Uttanasana — Standing Forward Fold (return)
      text: Exhale and fold forward over the legs. Hands toward the floor, head heavy.
    - name: Urdhva Hastasana — Upward Salute (return)
      text: Inhale and rise to standing, sweeping the arms overhead. Stack the spine vertebra by vertebra.
    - name: Tadasana — Mountain Pose (return)
      text: Exhale and lower the arms to your sides. Stand still for three breaths before the next round.
