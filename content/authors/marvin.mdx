## About Marvin

Marvin runs The Yoga Sensei. He is a long-time practitioner who does not teach yoga — this site is a gear-and-guide editorial, not a yoga school.

His writing is grounded in publicly available specifications, manufacturer documentation, and aggregated user reviews. When he has personally used a product, he says so. When he has not, he says that too.

## What Marvin writes about

- Gear reviews — mats, blocks, straps, props, apparel
- Beginner-path articles based on long-time practitioner experience
- Curated yoga apps, books, and online resources
- Pinterest-friendly visual guides to common poses

## What Marvin does NOT write about

- Therapeutic prescriptions for injuries or conditions
- Yoga philosophy beyond what is in the public canon
- Teacher-training advice — there are better resources for that

For instructional content (alignment, sequencing, therapeutic), this site links out to credentialed sources rather than publishing its own.
