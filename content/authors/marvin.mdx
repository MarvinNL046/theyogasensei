## About Marvin

Marvin runs The Yoga Sensei. He buys, uses, and reviews yoga gear over months, not afternoons. He practices yoga but does not teach it — instructional content on this site is reviewed by certified teachers.

## What Marvin writes about

- Gear reviews — mats, blocks, straps, props, apparel
- Beginner-path articles based on long-time practitioner experience
- Curated yoga apps, books, and online resources
- Pinterest-friendly visual guides to common poses

## What Marvin does NOT write about

- Therapeutic prescriptions for injuries or conditions
- Yoga philosophy beyond what is in the public canon
- Teacher-training advice — there are better resources for that
