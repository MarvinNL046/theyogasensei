This is a free 28-day chair yoga plan for seniors. It runs four weeks, starts at about six minutes a day, and builds to a full seated round of roughly fifteen. Everything is on this page — no sign-up, no email, no PDF to download. Print it if that helps, and tick the days off as you go.

The plan uses the twelve poses from our [chair yoga for seniors guide](/guides/chair-yoga-for-seniors), sequenced so that nothing arrives before you have practised the pieces it is built from. That is the whole design: it is our own ordering of moves we already teach, not a clinical programme.

This is educational movement guidance, not medical advice. Check with your doctor first if you have a health condition, a recent surgery, or balance problems, and stop anything that hurts.

By [Marvin Smit](/about) · Long-time practitioner, not a certified instructor.

## Before you start

You need one thing: a sturdy dining chair with four legs and no wheels, low enough that your feet rest flat on the floor. Not an armchair, not an office chair.

A few habits that make the month easier:

- **Pick a time and keep it.** Same chair, same part of the day. The plan is short enough to attach to something you already do, like after breakfast.
- **Sit forward, not back.** Perch toward the front of the seat with a hand's width behind you, unless a pose says otherwise.
- **Water within reach**, and stop if you feel dizzy or short of breath.
- **A lighter day is a real day.** Day 7 of each week is deliberately easy, and it counts.

## Week 1 — Settling in (about 6 minutes)

Four moves. The point of this week is not exercise, it is turning up.

| Day | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Seated Mountain · Shoulder Rolls · Ankle Circles · Rest and Breathing |
| 2 | Same four, one extra round of Shoulder Rolls |
| 3 | Same four, hold Seated Mountain for five slow breaths |
| 4 | Same four |
| 5 | Same four, add a second set of Ankle Circles |
| 6 | Same four, unhurried |
| 7 | **Lighter day:** Seated Mountain and Rest and Breathing only |

If any of these are new, read the how-to for each in the [main guide](/guides/chair-yoga-for-seniors) before you start — it covers the breath and the safety cue for every pose.

## Week 2 — Waking up the spine (about 9 minutes)

Three new moves join: Seated Cat-Cow, Seated Side Bend, and Heel and Toe Raises.

| Day | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| 8 | Week 1 four, then add Cat-Cow |
| 9 | Add Side Bend, both sides |
| 10 | All six, in order |
| 11 | All six, one extra round of Cat-Cow |
| 12 | Add Heel and Toe Raises |
| 13 | All seven, slowly |
| 14 | **Lighter day:** Mountain · Cat-Cow · Rest |

<Callout type="note" title="If a week feels like too much, repeat it">

There is nothing special about moving on when the calendar says so. Staying on Week 2 for a fortnight and then moving up is a better month than pushing into Week 3 and quietly stopping. The plan is a suggestion, not a test.

</Callout>

## Week 3 — Rotation and reach (about 12 minutes)

Three more: Seated Spinal Twist, Seated Eagle Arms, Seated Warrior Arms. This is the week where the everyday benefits usually start to show — turning to look behind you, reaching a shelf.

| Day | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | Week 2 seven, then add Spinal Twist, both sides |
| 16 | All eight, keeping the twist small |
| 17 | Add Eagle Arms |
| 18 | All nine, in order |
| 19 | Add Warrior Arms, holding a few breaths |
| 20 | All ten, unhurried |
| 21 | **Lighter day:** Mountain · Shoulder Rolls · Spinal Twist · Rest |

## Week 4 — The full round (about 15 minutes)

The last two arrive: Seated Forward Fold and the Seated Figure-Four Stretch. Skip the Figure-Four if you have a hip or knee replacement unless your professional has cleared it, and come up slowly from the Forward Fold.

| Day | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| 22 | Week 3 ten, then add Forward Fold |
| 23 | All eleven, coming up slowly |
| 24 | Add Figure-Four, both sides — or skip it and repeat Ankle Circles |
| 25 | The full twelve, in order |
| 26 | The full twelve, holding each a breath longer |
| 27 | The full twelve, as slowly as you can |
| 28 | **Your round:** the twelve, or only the ones you have come to like |

## What to do on day 29

Start again at Week 1, or go straight to the full round from Week 4 and treat it as your daily routine. Both work. The [printable chart](/guides/printable-chair-yoga-for-seniors) is the version to tape near the chair once you no longer need the day-by-day structure.

If you would rather follow along with someone on screen, our guide to [free chair yoga videos](/guides/free-chair-yoga-for-seniors) covers what to look for in a good one — and the pause button that makes home practice easier than a class.

## How to print this plan

Use your browser's print command — Ctrl+P on Windows, Cmd+P on a Mac. The four week tables print cleanly on paper. Tape the page somewhere you will see it in the morning, and use a pen: ticking a box is a surprisingly reliable way to keep a habit alive.
