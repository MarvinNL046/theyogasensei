faq:
  - q: How often should a beginner practice yoga?
    a: Aim for fifteen to twenty minutes daily rather than long weekly sessions. Consistency matters more than duration for habit formation and progress.
  - q: Do I need to be flexible to start yoga?
    a: No. Flexibility is a result of yoga, not a prerequisite. Beginners with limited range still benefit from day one.
  - q: How long until I see results?
    a: Most beginners report better sleep within two weeks and noticeable flexibility gains around the six-week mark, assuming a daily fifteen-minute practice.
  - q: What should I wear to my first yoga class?
    a: Anything you can move and sweat in comfortably. Stretchy leggings or shorts plus a fitted top. Avoid loose t-shirts that fall over your head in inversions.
  - q: Yoga or Pilates — which is better for beginners?
    a: Yoga builds flexibility and a meditative practice; Pilates builds core strength and postural control. Beginners with stress-related goals lean yoga; beginners with back-pain rehab lean Pilates. Many people end up doing both.
  - q: Is yoga a religion?
    a: No. Modern yoga as practised in studios is a physical and breath-focused exercise system. Its roots are in Indian philosophy, but no religious belief is required to practise.
