## YMYL And Trust Level

This is a higher-risk YMYL-adjacent topic because it involves older adults, mobility, balance, chronic conditions, and exercise safety.

Required safeguards:

- Use educational language, not medical advice.
- Add a medical disclaimer near the start and near the safety section.
- Use cautious wording: "may support," "can help some people," "is often used for," not "fixes," "cures," or "prevents."
- Require source-backed claims for balance, blood pressure, sleep, anxiety, cognition, fall prevention, or arthritis.
- Do not claim medical review, RYT credentials, senior fitness credentials, or healthcare review unless that person actually reviewed the page.
- If no credentialed reviewer is available, say the article is researched and cite reliable sources, but do not fake credentials.
