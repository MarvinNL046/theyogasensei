## Narrative integrity notes applied

- Manduka GRP Adapt 2.0 is the only mat in this current lineup that Manduka themselves positions as hot-yoga/sweat-ready. The eKO and eKO Lite are all-round natural-rubber mats that can work for hot yoga, but the article should not imply they are Manduka's dedicated hot-yoga mats.
- Jade Harmony's open-cell rubber is useful for grip but absorbs more moisture, so hygiene/cleaning trade-off must be named.
- Yogitoes vs eQua is a grip-mechanism distinction, not simply premium vs budget: Yogitoes anchors with silicone nubs; eQua relies more on moisture-activated microfiber grip.
- Manduka Mat Wash is included as a care synergy CTA, not as a core ranking pick.
