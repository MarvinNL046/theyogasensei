> **How thick should a beginner yoga mat be?**
> Four to five millimetres is the sweet spot for most beginners. Thinner travel mats around three millimetres can be tough on the knees during low lunges, while mats thicker than six millimetres make standing balance poses harder than they need to be.

> **Are expensive yoga mats worth it for a beginner?**
> For the first six months of practice, a mid-priced mat that grips well when you sweat will teach you more than a premium mat that costs twice as much. Upgrade once you know what you do and do not like about your current mat.
