## The five takeaways

1. **Pinterest ≠ Google.** Two of our bets — **hot yoga** and **chair yoga** — have ~no Pinterest demand. They're Google/Facebook plays. Don't expect Pinterest distribution for them, and weigh this in the next-pillar decision (chair-yoga = Google/seniors, not Pinterest).
2. **Our money cluster IS the Pinterest sweet spot.** yoga mat + yoga for beginners are exactly where Pinterest volume is — and we already have those pages + pins live. Good alignment.
3. **Audience = young women 18-34 (~85–88% female).** Aesthetic-first pins win (validated by "...aesthetic" terms trending). Our calm editorial pin style fits perfectly — lean into it. Match copy tone to this audience.
4. **Seasonality is the timing lever.**
   - yoga mat + yoga for beginners → **Dec–Jan peak** → build/seed the backlog **now through autumn** so pins are mature before the surge (pins take weeks to gain traction).
   - yoga poses → **spring peak** → seed pose pins late winter.
5. **Biggest untapped opportunity: the poses cluster.** "yoga poses" is a huge, inherently save-worthy visual category we've barely built (only sun-salutation live). Pose pins + the young aesthetic audience = a double win (Google + Pinterest). This argues for **prioritising the poses cluster over chair-yoga** as a next build.

## How we use it — action plan

**A. Optimise pin copy to the searched phrases (Pinterest = search engine).**
- Title/describe pins with the exact terms people search, not just our guide titles. E.g. the `how-to-store-a-yoga-mat` pin → lead title **"Yoga Mat Storage Ideas"** (matches "yoga mat storage"). The eco pins → "aesthetic eco yoga mat". Beginner pins → "yoga for beginners".
- Make **multiple pin variants per guide**, each targeting a different related phrase (we already generate 5 angles/guide — re-title a couple to these phrases).

**B. Time the pushes.**
- **Now → November:** ramp mat + beginner pins (3–5/week per the launch schedule) so they're established before the Dec–Jan resolution surge.
- **Jan → spring:** push pose pins as the poses cluster comes online.

**C. Reprioritise the content roadmap (proposal).**
- Bump the **poses cluster** up (downward-dog already briefed; sun-salutation live) — strong on both Google and Pinterest.
- Treat **chair-yoga** as a Google/Facebook play if/when built — don't expect Pinterest reach.

**D. Study winners.** Use the "Populaire pins" panel on each Trends term to see what top-performing pins look like (format, text density, colour) and mirror what works.

**E. Measure.** In ~1–2 weeks, check Pinterest Analytics (own pins) + GSC to see which guides get clicks, and feed the winners more pins.
