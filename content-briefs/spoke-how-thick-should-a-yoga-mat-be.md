## Required angle

The article must make the conflict useful:

- 3mm is not wrong — it is for firm feedback, portability and flow.
- 4–5mm is the general-purpose answer for most home practitioners.
- 6mm is a cushion-forward yoga mat, especially for hard floors and sensitive knees.
- 8–10mm is usually restorative/floor-work territory, not the best default for standing yoga.
- Density can matter more than thickness: a dense 4mm mat can outperform a soft 6mm mat.
- Many “I need a thicker mat” cases are actually “I need a knee pad” cases.
