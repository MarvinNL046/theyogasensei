## Required answer and structure

1. Open with the four-step D-ring answer in fewer than 70 words.
2. Show a labelled equipment check before any threading steps.
3. Numbered D-ring method:
   - feed the free end through both rings;
   - return it through one ring to form an adjustable loop;
   - place and tighten the loop around one end of the rolled mat;
   - form or position the second carrier loop according to the strap’s construction, keeping the middle section as the handle.
4. Separate instructions for a ready-made two-loop carrier. Do not imply every strap converts in the same way.
5. Troubleshooting table: mat slides out, loop jams, mat unrolls, strap twists, metal ring touches the mat surface.
6. Explain when a bag or purpose-built carrier is the safer answer.
7. FAQ: use without rings, use a practice strap as a carrier, ideal strap length and whether tight loops damage a mat.
