### What Is Downward Facing Dog?

- Basic shape: inverted V.
- Common use in yoga flows, sun salutations, transitions, and resting moments.
- Why beginners meet it early.

### How to Do Downward Facing Dog Step by Step

1. Start on hands and knees.
2. Place hands shoulder-width apart and spread fingers.
3. Tuck toes and lift hips.
4. Keep knees bent at first.
5. Press through hands while lengthening the spine.
6. Let heels move toward the floor without forcing them.
7. Breathe steadily.

### Alignment Cues That Actually Help

- Press through thumb and index finger base.
- Soften the knees to protect the spine shape.
- Lift hips up and back.
- Keep neck relaxed.
- Think long spine before straight legs.

### Benefits of Downward Facing Dog

Use cautious language:

- May help stretch calves, hamstrings, shoulders, and back body.
- Can build upper-body and core awareness.
- Supports body awareness and breath rhythm.
- Helps connect poses in many beginner routines.

### Common Mistakes

- Forcing heels to the floor.
- Rounding the spine to straighten legs.
- Collapsing into wrists.
- Shrugging shoulders toward ears.
- Holding the breath.
- Treating it as a passive rest pose too early.

### Beginner Modifications

- Bend knees generously.
- Practice with hands on blocks.
- Shorten or widen stance.
- Try puppy pose as a gentler alternative.
- Rest in child’s pose when wrists or shoulders feel overloaded.

### Who Should Be Careful

- Wrist, shoulder, neck, back, or blood pressure concerns.
- Pregnancy or dizziness considerations.
- Include educational disclaimer: not medical advice; consult a qualified professional when needed.

### How Long Should You Hold It?

- Beginners: 2 to 4 breaths.
- Build gradually.
- Quality over duration.
