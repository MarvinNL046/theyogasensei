# Digital PR — Link Building & Brand Authority

Companion to [outreach-plan.md](./outreach-plan.md). Source: notes from Ashish Kakar on digital PR as a link-building strategy. (Canonical copy lives in the Obsidian ContentOps vault.)

**Core idea:** journalists don't link to "good SEO content" — they link to something **newsworthy, useful, or citable**. Digital PR earns strong backlinks _and_ brand authority (entity signals) by giving media something worth referencing.

## Two modes

### 1. Reactive PR — easy start, competitive

Respond to journalist requests looking for expert quotes. Cheap, fast, beginner-friendly, but crowded.

- **Platforms:** Qwoted, Featured, Source of Sources (SOS), #JournoRequest on X. _(These are already the Tier-2 rows in [outreach-targets.csv](./outreach-targets.csv).)_
- ⚠️ **Correction:** the source mentions _Connectively / HARO_ — that **shut down Dec 2024**. Featured.com bought the HARO brand (revived as a free newsletter). Use the platforms above (verified 2026).
- **Speed > perfection** — reply fast, don't wait to polish.

### 2. Active / proactive PR — stronger

You create a newsworthy **linkable asset** and pitch it. Better links, more referral traffic, more relevant mentions.

- **Asset types:** survey, original research, dataset, data-driven article, infographic, tool.
- **Surveys work best** — especially if repeated **yearly** (becomes a recurring, citable source → compounding).
- Also builds your brand as an **entity** (matters for AI/LLM visibility). Unlinked brand mentions still have value.
- **Press releases:** weak for durable backlinks, but can shape the online narrative + AI visibility.

## Workflow (active PR)

1. Pick a topic broad enough for journalists, relevant enough for your brand.
2. Build a linkable asset (survey / research / data / infographic / tool).
3. Find journalists who wrote on similar topics in the last **60–90 days**.
4. Build a media list: name, email, publication, the relevant article.
5. Tools: Muck Rack, Hunter, BuzzStream, Pitchbox, NinjaOutreach.
6. Short pitch, subject line **~5–7 words**.
7. Personalise using the journalist's earlier article.
8. Send at smart times (~**11:00 local**).
9. Follow up after a few days.
10. Repeat weekly/monthly until you have enough links.

## Case study

A client ran a **survey on TikTok use among teens** → pitched to media → links from **Times of India, WorldHealth.net**, etc. Those articles then earned their _own_ links (natural **tiered link building**). Result: more rankings + **~30% more GSC keywords** around TikTok-related terms.

## Takeaways

- Reactive PR: **speed beats perfection**.
- Surveys cost more but are often the **best investment** (repeatable = compounding authority).
- **Vary anchor text**, include your brand name.
- Make content interesting **beyond your direct audience** (widens journalist appeal).
- Real value = **authority**: others cite/mention/link you because you add something unique.

- This is the **step up** beyond the reactive + guest-post + resource-page rows in the outreach plan.
- For a DR-0 young site: start **reactive** (Qwoted/Featured/SOS) for quick wins → then build **one repeatable survey/data asset per niche** (yoga gear; sleep/pillows; duvets; saws/DIY) as the active-PR flagship.
- Linkable assets already built: the free **printable chair-yoga** + **Starter Guide** (theyogasensei); wattbespaarjij's **terugverdientijd-/besparingscalculator**. A survey or data piece is the next-level asset.
